How the agent works and what it never does

The agent uses its own browser with an empty profile, so none of your sign-ins or cookies. It hovers and clicks like a person but never types or submits. A click that would leave the page is stopped, and where it would have gone is recorded. Nothing is sent to CyberForge, and addresses on your computer or local network are not visited.
