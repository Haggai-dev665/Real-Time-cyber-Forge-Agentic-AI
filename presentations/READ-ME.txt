THESIS
The original root file, Che Haggai Ngha Kang.docx, is now revised.
Che Haggai Ngha Kang - Original backup.docx in this folder is the untouched source.
The original title, authorship and academic date were preserved.
117 original paragraphs were revised; Appendix C was added; architecture and
decision-flow figures were redrawn. Historical results tables were preserved,
except the summary table's latency target was corrected to the stricter target
in the original methods chapter. The 4.05-second mean is 0.05 seconds above it.
Word is instructed to update fields when opening. If the contents/page numbers
are not refreshed automatically, update the table of contents using Update
entire table. The final pagination must be checked in Word before submission.

SUBSTANTIVE UPDATES
- Tauri 2/Rust replaces the obsolete Electron desktop description.
- Node.js 24/Express, Python 3.11/FastAPI REST, Expo/React Native and shared Rust
  CLI/MCP source are described, with their actual scope and release limitations.
- Three supervised gradient-boosted URL classifiers, benign-only IsolationForest,
  pretrained BERT/Qwen inference, DGA/IOC rules and memory retrieval are separated.
- Training phases, domain-grouped partitions, threshold selection, acceptance
  gates and publishing are documented. Training currently shares inference.
- Consent, workspace ownership, service authentication, signed commands, explicit
  action receipts and capability-aware console states are documented.
- The original Gemini/Mistral experiment is distinguished from current Qwen.
- The current Reporter weighted sum is distinguished from the historical 60/40
  equation and from the newer versioned detector decision policy.
- MCP stdio exists in source but the desktop Integrations UI still says it is
  not built; that mismatch and packaged end-to-end acceptance remain outstanding.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
The original 300-URL results were not rerun. They are historical findings, not
fresh performance claims about the revised source. No new training, production
deployment, cloud availability measurement or hardware benchmark was performed.
The original screenshots are labelled historical illustrations; their visible
counters are not additional experimental data. Memory does not retrain weights.
